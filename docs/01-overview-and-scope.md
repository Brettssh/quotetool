# 01 · Overview and scope

Status: In review · Owner: Brett Price · Decided in scope session 1, 2026-11-24

## Product
SCOPE-01. A quote and contract tool inside Zoho that replaces D-Tools for proposals.

## Users
SCOPE-02. SmartShield Homes staff only: sales/design, project managers, bookkeeper, warehouse, crew leads. Each user signs in with their own Zoho account.
SCOPE-03. Expected user count: 5 to 7.

## Customers and contracts
SCOPE-04. Contract customers are builders and homeowners. A job (house) can have a builder contract only, a homeowner contract only, or both.
SCOPE-05. Jobs with no builder (for example, a direct homeowner retrofit or service work) are in scope and have one homeowner contract.

## In scope
SCOPE-06. **Quoting:** catalog, price books per builder or tier, accessories and labor added automatically, base quotes with revisions, change orders, configurators (lighting first), proposals e-signed in Zoho Sign.
SCOPE-07. **Contract and billing, including change orders:** the Deal is the contract; draw schedule; invoices created from the Deal; each accepted change order adds to the contract value and carries its own draws; contract summary for the customer.
SCOPE-08. **Purchasing:** purchase orders are generated by netting what the contract needs against inventory on hand. Ordered and received status is tracked by job, not by room.
SCOPE-09. **Field and warehouse:** kits per install day, staging, delivery marking by crew leads, inventory moves. This area overlaps the separate **Zoho Inventory turn-up** project; the boundary between the two is agreed later.
SCOPE-10. **Reports:** in scope; contents defined in their own requirements session.
SCOPE-11. **Vendor catalog feeds:** keeping catalog prices and product data current from vendors without D-Tools. Routes known today:

| Vendor | Route |
|---|---|
| Lutron | Pricing file downloaded from Lutron online (MyProjects) |
| Sony | Spreadsheet |
| Sonos | Spreadsheet |
| Sanus | Spreadsheet |
| ADI | ADI API |
| Silmar | To be decided |
| Amazon | Manual entry |
| Home Depot | Manual entry |

## Systems
SCOPE-12. In scope: Zoho CRM, Zoho Books / Zoho Inventory, Zoho Sign, Zoho Writer, Zoho Analytics, and Zoho Catalyst where Deluge cannot do the job.
SCOPE-13. D-Tools: a one-time export of products, labor hours and accessories; afterwards D-Tools is used only for catalog updates until the vendor catalog feeds replace it.

## Out of scope
SCOPE-14. Accounting setup in Zoho Books (chart of accounts, tax setup) stays as it is; the tool creates invoices and purchase orders and reads payments only.
SCOPE-15. Other company products stay separate for now: Alarm.com, the Field Reference tool, the lighting design tool.
SCOPE-16. No customer portal: customers receive PDFs and Zoho Sign emails.
SCOPE-17. No crew scheduling or time tracking; a kit carries an install date only.

## Delivery
SCOPE-18. Delivered in phases, each usable on its own: (1) quote and contract, (2) purchasing and BOM, (3) field and warehouse, plus a vendor catalog feeds phase. D-Tools is retired for proposals after phase 1 (after one real job runs side by side with the old process) and fully once the vendor catalog feeds phase is live.

## Deferred and open
SCOPE-19. Configurators in Zoho Creator or CRM guided selling: deferred to design.
SCOPE-20. Silmar catalog route: to be decided.
